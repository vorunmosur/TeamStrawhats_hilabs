Measured performance, with every held-out row counted

Three supplied folds. Models exclude their held-out fold. Headline accuracy is the lower of the two system accuracies.

What these results mean

Internal out-of-fold validation is separate from final-model predictions and hidden evaluation. Rare and unseen codes remain in the denominator. Candidate recall measures whether ranking could possibly select the correct code.

No manual evaluation mappings, no gold candidates inserted at validation, and no abstention used to inflate accuracy.

A complete, offline mapping path

All 480,306 supplied concepts remain searchable, including codes absent from labelled training examples.

01 | INPUT
Entity + context

03 | RANKING
Learned context signals

Qualifier features and fold-isolated example memory.

04 | OUTPUT
Two legal codes
